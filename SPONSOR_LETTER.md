## Detailed Version

Subject: Sponsoring a Fireseed Alpha project for verifiable organization identity continuity

Hello,

I am reaching out about OrgAnchor, an open-source project designed to reduce the cost of verifying organizational identity continuity and evidence-bearing public claims.

The core problem is simple: organizations increasingly live through domains, websites, platforms, cloud providers, archives, and mirrors. These carriers are useful, but fragile. At the same time, AI-generated media and polished pages make appearance cheaper than evidence. For people and AI agents, the important question becomes:

- Who is speaking?
- Is this the same organization over time?
- Which endpoint is official now?
- Which claims are signed?
- Which evidence supports them?
- What is stale, missing, contradicted, or outside scope?
- What should be checked next before a transaction, partnership, listing, or safety decision?

OrgAnchor's answer is to make the verification path explicit:

- Organization root authority.
- Signed official endpoint statement.
- Public `/verify` package.
- Machine-readable `/.well-known/organchor.json`.
- AI-agent compact verification output.
- Signed claims and evidence manifests.
- Carrier receipts for web, IPFS, Arweave, OpenTimestamps/Bitcoin, Onion, ENS, and Directory experiments.

The current Fireseed Alpha is a serious seed, not a finished trust infrastructure. It demonstrates the minimum useful loop and exposes current limits. In particular, S1-S3 evidence has an alpha baseline, while S4/S5 real-use observation and public challenge remain design-preview areas that need external critique and co-building.

We are seeking sponsorship for:

- Producing public explanation and demo materials.
- Supporting the first low-risk external pilot.
- Funding independent technical review.
- Funding evidence/governance review.
- Improving AI-agent and Directory discovery compatibility.
- Maintaining package integrity, examples, and adopter documentation.

Important boundary:

Sponsorship must not buy ranking, certification, verification outcomes, Directory priority, trust status, or influence over whether another organization passes or fails a check. OrgAnchor should remain a protocol and tooling project that makes evidence more inspectable, not a paid trust gate.
