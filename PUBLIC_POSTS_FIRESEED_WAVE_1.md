## LinkedIn Post

```text
OrgAnchor Fireseed Alpha is ready for public review.

AI-generated pages, videos, and product materials are making appearance cheaper than evidence. At the same time, real organizations still depend on fragile carriers: domains, websites, platform accounts, cloud providers, and scattered public records.

OrgAnchor is an open-source project for helping organizations publish signed, recheckable public records that link identity, official presence, claims, evidence, and migration history so people and AI agents can discover, screen, verify, understand, and compare candidate organizations at lower cost.

It is not a trust badge, not a marketplace, not a certification authority, and not a final ranking system.

The current Fireseed Alpha includes a visible minimum loop: root authority, signed official-presence records, public /verify package, Beacon discovery, compact AI-agent verification, signed claims/evidence, and S1-S3 evidence baseline.

We are looking for early reviewers, pilot organizations, AI-agent builders, Directory builders, and evidence/governance critics.
```
